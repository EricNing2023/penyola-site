Beijing in 3 days: a first-time itinerary

Last checked: 9 November 2026

Is 3 days enough for Beijing?

Yes, for the essentials: the Forbidden City and Jingshan Park on day 1, the Great Wall on day 2, and the Temple of Heaven and the Summer Palace on day 3. It is a full plan with early starts. The one thing to fix first is the Forbidden City, which sells out: tickets go on sale at 20:00 Beijing time seven days ahead.

Which days should I avoid?

Mondays. The Forbidden City is closed on Mondays, and so are the halls inside the Temple of Heaven, except on public holidays. If your trip includes a Monday, make it your Great Wall day.

Day 1: the Forbidden City and Jingshan Park

  • Morning Be at the Meridian Gate for the 08:30 opening. Walk the central axis north, with one side trip: the half-day route takes 3–4 hours. Check the entrance and exits before you set off.
  • Lunch Out through the Gate of Divine Might, the north exit.
  • Afternoon Cross the road to Jingshan Park and climb to Wanchun Pavilion for the view back over the palace roofs.
  • Late afternoon Walk north into the hutong lanes around Shichahai and the Drum and Bell Towers.

Day 2: the Great Wall

  • Early start Leave by 07:30. Mutianyu, about 70 km away, opens at 07:30 from mid-March to mid-November and at 08:00 in winter. See which section to choose.
  • On the wall Allow two to three hours. Take the cable car up if you want to save the climb.
  • Back in town A quiet evening: you will be tired. Peking duck is a good reward.

Day 3: the Temple of Heaven and the Summer Palace

  • Early morning The Temple of Heaven park opens at 06:00 (06:30 from November to March), and in the early morning it is full of locals doing tai chi, dancing and playing cards. The halls open at 08:00: the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, the Echo Wall and the Circular Mound Altar. Buy the combined ticket (¥34, or ¥28 from November to March) to see them. Metro: Line 5 to Tiantandongmen, Exit A.
  • Afternoon Cross the city to the Summer Palace in the northwest. Walk the Long Corridor along Kunming Lake to the Marble Boat, and climb to the Tower of Buddhist Incense if you have the energy.
  • Evening Back to the centre for dinner.

What if I have more time, or less?

  • 2 days Drop the Temple of Heaven and pair the Great Wall with the Summer Palace on the way back; see Beijing in 2 days.
  • 4 days or more Give the Summer Palace a whole day, and spend a morning in the hutongs and an afternoon at the Lama Temple or Beihai Park.

How do I get around?

The metro covers every stop on this plan except the Great Wall, and is signed in English. Taxis and DiDi are cheap for short hops; carry each place's name in Chinese. See Beijing without Google for payments and apps, and address cards to show drivers.
