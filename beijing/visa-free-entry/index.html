First time in Beijing · Before you fly

Can I visit Beijing without a visa?

Last checked: 23 November 2026

Probably, if you hold an ordinary passport from one of two lists. Citizens of 50 countries, including the UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, Korea and most of the EU, can stay up to 30 days for tourism with no visa. US citizens are not on that list, but they and citizens of 56 other countries can use 240-hour visa-free transit: up to 10 days in China on the way to a third country, arriving at Beijing Capital or Beijing Daxing airport. For 48 of the 50 countries, the 30-day waiver currently runs until 31 December 2026. Brunei has no end date and Russia runs to 31 December 2027.

If you qualify for both, the 30-day waiver is simpler: there is no transit condition, it works at any open port and it gives you longer.

Which countries get 30 days without a visa?

Ordinary passport holders of these 50 countries: Andorra, Argentina, Australia, Austria, Bahrain, Belgium, Brazil, Brunei, Bulgaria, Canada, Chile, Croatia, Cyprus, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Japan, the Republic of Korea, Kuwait, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro, the Netherlands, New Zealand, North Macedonia, Norway, Oman, Peru, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Russia, Saudi Arabia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and Uruguay.

The waiver covers business, tourism, visiting family or friends, exchanges and transit. It does not cover work, study or journalism. Children follow the same rules as adults, and tour groups and independent travellers are treated the same.

What are the rules for the 30-day waiver?

  • You need an ordinary passport valid for at least the length of your stay. Travel documents and emergency passports do not qualify.
  • The 30 days start the day after you enter and run for 30 calendar days.
  • You can fly in from any country, and enter at any sea, land or air port open to foreigners.
  • You can use it again on later trips. There is currently no limit on the number of entries or total days.
  • You do not need to register with an embassy beforehand.
  • Border officers decide on entry. The ministry recommends carrying papers that match your trip, such as your air tickets and hotel bookings.
  • To stay longer than 30 days, get a visa before you travel.

What happens after 31 December 2026?

For 48 of the 50 countries, the ministry's page gives 31 December 2026 as the end date, and no extension had been announced when we checked on 23 November 2026. If your trip runs into 2027, check the Ministry of Foreign Affairs page linked below before you book, or ask your nearest Chinese embassy. We recheck it every week and will update this page when it changes.

How does 240-hour visa-free transit work?

Citizens of 57 countries can enter China without a visa when they are passing through to a third country or region:

  • Carry an ordinary passport and a confirmed onward ticket with a fixed date and itinerary, to a third country or region. Beijing back to the country you came from is not transit.
  • Arrive through one of 65 listed ports in 24 provinces. In Beijing these are Beijing Capital International Airport and Beijing Daxing International Airport.
  • Stay up to 10 days. Arriving in Beijing, you can stay in the Beijing municipality; the immigration administration says transit travellers can also move between the permitted areas of the 24 provinces within the 240 hours.
  • Tourism, business, visits and family visits are allowed. Work, study and journalism need a visa.

The 57 countries:

  • Europe (40): Albania, Austria, Belarus, Belgium, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, the Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco, Montenegro, the Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Russia, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Ukraine and the United Kingdom.
  • Americas (6): Argentina, Brazil, Canada, Chile, Mexico and the United States.
  • Oceania (2): Australia and New Zealand.
  • Asia (9): Brunei, Indonesia, Japan, Kyrgyzstan, Qatar, the Republic of Korea, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates and Vietnam.

What about a short layover?

All open ports allow 24-hour transit without a visa for travellers of any nationality who stay in the airport's restricted area and have a confirmed onward ticket. To leave the airport, you need a temporary entry permit from the border inspection officers.
