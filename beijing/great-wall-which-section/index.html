Which section of the Great Wall should I visit from Beijing?

Last checked: 9 November 2026

Which section is best for a first visit?

For most first-time visitors, Mutianyu: about 70 km from central Beijing, a restored wall with watchtowers along a wooded ridge, and a cable car that saves the steep climb. Badaling, about 60 km northwest, is the easiest to reach by public transport, by train. Juyongguan is a fortified pass with tickets at the gate, and Simatai is the one you can walk at night.

Mutianyu: what do I need to know?

  • Tickets are sold by real-name booking, up to 30 days ahead; book with your passport and show the same passport at the gate. Each ID can book one ticket per day.
  • The wall ticket is ¥40. The shuttle bus between the service area and the wall, a 6 km round trip, is ¥15 and recommended. The cable car is ¥140 return or ¥100 one way. The chairlift and toboggan are run by a separate company and need their own tickets.
  • Opening hours: 16 March–15 November, 07:30–18:00 on weekdays and 07:30–18:30 at weekends; 16 November–15 March, 08:00–17:30. Ticket sales stop an hour before closing and entry half an hour before.
  • Towers 1 to 20 are open by day.
  • You can cancel or change a booking until midnight on the day of your visit, as long as you have not entered.
  • By public transport: bus 916 from Dongzhimen to Huairou Beidajie, then bus H23, H3 or H71 to Mutianyujingqu.

Badaling: what do I need to know?

  • Opening hours: 1 April–31 October, 06:30–16:30; 1 November–31 March, 07:30–16:00. The open section runs from North Tower 12 to South Tower 7.5.
  • Booking ahead is encouraged, through the official Chinese-language channels, and tickets are also sold at the gate, including on public holidays.
  • Getting there: the S2 suburban line or the Beijing–Zhangjiakou high-speed railway, or buses 919, 880 and 877. Check the railway timetable for the day.

Juyongguan: what do I need to know?

Juyongguan is a pass in a valley in Changping District, where the wall climbs steeply up both hillsides from a fortified gate. Tickets are sold at the gate: ¥40 in peak season, ¥35 off season. Hours are 08:30–17:30 in peak season and 08:30–16:30 off season.

Simatai: can I walk the Great Wall at night?

Yes, at Simatai, which is entered through Gubei Water Town, a resort in Miyun District about 120 km and two hours' drive from central Beijing. Night visits run all year:

  • May–October: 18:00–21:40.
  • November–April: 17:30–20:10 from Sunday to Thursday, 17:30–20:40 on Friday and Saturday.
  • By day, Simatai is open 09:00–17:00; entry closes at 16:10 from inside the water town and at 15:00 at the outside gate.
  • Tickets: Simatai ¥40 by day; the Great Wall at night ¥200; Gubei Water Town ¥140.
  • By public transport: bus 980 (express) from Dongzhimen to Miyun Xidaqiao, then bus Mi 51 towards Simatai.

Jinshanling, across the border in Hebei, is popular with hikers. We could not confirm its current tickets and hours on an official site, so it is not covered here.

How do I get there without a tour?

The simplest is a car with a driver for the day, booked through your hotel or a ride-hailing app. For Badaling the train is easy. For Mutianyu and Simatai, public buses work but take a change and most of a morning. Save the Chinese name of the section to show your driver: Mutianyu 慕田峪长城, Badaling 八达岭长城, Juyongguan 居庸关长城, Simatai 司马台长城.

How long should I allow?

A full day, or a morning and early afternoon: about half the day on the road there and back, and two to three hours on the wall. Start early, especially in summer and at weekends. Bring water, and shoes with grip: the steps are steep and uneven. Do not plan the Forbidden City for the same day.
