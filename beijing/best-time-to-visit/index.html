First time in Beijing · Beijing · timing

When is the best time to visit Beijing?

Last checked: 23 November 2026

Spring and autumn, outside China's public holidays. Beijing's springs and autumns are short, its summers hot and rainy and its winters cold and dry, so April to May and September to October are the comfortable months. The catch is that the two biggest holidays fall in them: in 2027, avoid 1–5 May and 1–7 October. Winter is the budget choice: the Forbidden City costs ¥40 instead of ¥60 from November to March, but the sights close earlier.

What is Beijing's weather like through the year?

The city government describes a temperate, continental monsoon climate: short springs and autumns, hot and rainy summers, cold and dry winters. The average annual temperature is 11–14 °C. About three-quarters of the year's rain falls in summer, most of it in July and August.

How do opening hours and prices change with the seasons?

Most of the big sights switch to winter hours on 1 November and back on 1 April. The Great Wall at Mutianyu switches on its own dates.

The Forbidden City, the halls inside the Temple of Heaven and the Summer Palace's inner gardens close on Mondays, except on public holidays. The parks themselves open every day.

What changes in each season at the sights?

  • Spring and midsummer: Jingshan Park holds cultural seasons, usually from mid or late April to May and from mid or late July to August, when the adult ticket rises from ¥2 to ¥10.
  • Boating season: the Summer Palace boats on Kunming Lake stop for the winter; in 2026 they stopped on 2 November. In season, the boat from the Marble Boat to South Lake Island costs ¥40.
  • Around the winter solstice: the setting sun lights up the inside of the Seventeen-Arch Bridge at the Summer Palace.
  • Winter afternoons: last entry to the Forbidden City is 15:30, so start in the morning. See the Forbidden City in winter.

What is the best day of the week?

Tuesday to Thursday. Mondays close the Forbidden City and the halls at the Temple of Heaven and the Summer Palace, and weekends are busier everywhere. If your trip includes a Monday, make it your Great Wall day. For hour-by-hour advice at the palace, see Forbidden City crowds.
