First time in Beijing · Arriving

How do I get from Beijing airport to the city centre?

Last checked: 9 November 2026

What is the easiest way into the city?

The airport express train. From Capital Airport (PEK), the Capital Airport Express runs from Terminals 3 and 2 to Sanyuanqiao, Dongzhimen and Beixinqiao for a flat ¥25, about every 10 minutes. From Daxing Airport (PKX), the Daxing Airport Express runs to Caoqiao in the south of the city for ¥35. Both end at metro stations where you can change lines. With heavy bags, or after the last train, take a taxi from the official taxi rank.

Which airport am I landing at?

Check the airport code on your ticket. PEK is Beijing Capital International Airport, northeast of the centre, with Terminals 2 and 3. PKX is Beijing Daxing International Airport, far to the south. They are different airports with different trains, so make sure you plan for the right one.

Fares and times are the airports' own figures when we checked. On the Daxing line, ticket sales stop five minutes before the last train, and the airport suggests being at the station ten minutes before it.

Capital Airport (PEK): what else should I know?

  • Stations The line has five: Beixinqiao, Dongzhimen, Sanyuanqiao, Terminal 3 and Terminal 2. Going back to the airport, the first trains leave Beixinqiao at 05:56 and Dongzhimen at 06:00; the last leave at 22:26 and 22:30.
  • Taxis T3: the taxi area on level B1. T2: level 1, the middle lane outside doors 5 to 9. Use the official rank only.
  • Ride-hailing Pick-ups are in the parking buildings: for T3, parking building 3, level B2, zone H; for T2, parking building 2, level 1, south-east side.
  • Airport buses A 24-hour bus runs to Beijing West Railway Station, ¥25–30 depending on the stop, and another goes to Beijing South Railway Station, ¥20–30. Buses leave from outside door 1 on level 1 of T3 and door 11 on level 1 of T2.

Daxing Airport (PKX): what else should I know?

  • Into town The express stops at Daxing Xincheng, then Caoqiao, where you change to metro Lines 10 or 19.
  • Children Children 1.3 m tall or under ride free with a paying adult.
  • Air–rail tickets China Southern, China Eastern, Air China and Xiamen Airlines offer discounted express tickets to some passengers flying from or to Daxing. The rules vary; check with your airline.
  • Flying out At Caoqiao you can check in, drop your bags and have them screened before you ride to the airport.

How do I get to the Forbidden City from the airport?

Take the airport express to the metro, then the metro to Tiananmen East or Tiananmen West on Line 1, the stations the museum lists for the Meridian Gate, where visitors enter. Palace tickets go on sale online at 20:00 Beijing time seven days ahead and are never sold on the day, so book before you fly: see how to book with a passport and the entrance and exits.

What should I sort out before I land?

A way to pay on your phone and a way to get online. Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay with your card before you fly; see Beijing without Google.
