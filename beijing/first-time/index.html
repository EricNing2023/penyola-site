First time in Beijing

Last checked: 10 November 2026

A first trip to Beijing goes smoothly if you sort out three things before you fly: payments on your phone, a way to get online, and your Forbidden City booking, which opens seven days ahead and sells out. The guides below follow the order of a trip, from before you land to planning each day.

The short version

  • Set up Alipay or WeChat Pay with your card and test it before you travel; see Beijing without Google.
  • Book the Forbidden City at 20:00 Beijing time, seven days ahead, with your passport; see how to book.
  • Carry the Chinese address of every place you are going; see address cards.
  • Keep your passport with you: it is your ticket at the big sights.
