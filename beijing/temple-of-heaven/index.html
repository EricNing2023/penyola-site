Temple of Heaven: tickets, hours and how to visit

Last checked: 9 November 2026

Do I need to book the Temple of Heaven?

Book ahead if you can. Tickets are sold by real-name booking 1–7 days in advance, and foreign visitors book on the "Temple of Heaven" WeChat account with their passport. Get the combined ticket, ¥34 from April to October or ¥28 from November to March, to go into the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, the Echo Wall and the Circular Mound Altar. These sights close on Mondays, except on public holidays, while the park itself is open every day.

The ticketed sights are the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, the Echo Wall, the Circular Mound Altar, the Fasting Palace, the Divine Kitchen, the Animal Sacrifice Pavilion and the Divine Music Administration. All of them close on Mondays, except on public holidays.

How do foreign visitors book?

  • Book on the "Temple of Heaven" WeChat account with your passport, 1–7 days ahead, for a time slot. The e-ticket is valid only on the day you booked.
  • At the gate, show the e-ticket QR code and the passport you booked with. Save a screenshot of the QR code before you go.
  • If the main sights have not sold out, you can buy same-day tickets for them by scanning the QR code at the entrance, and the service counters in the park can also book for you.
  • Unused tickets can be refunded free of charge within 7 days of the visit date, on the platform you booked through. Used tickets cannot be refunded.

What is there to see?

The Temple of Heaven is where the Ming and Qing emperors offered sacrifices to heaven and prayed for good harvests. The park covers 273 hectares inside a double wall. Its northern end is rounded and its southern end square, after the old belief that heaven is round and the earth square.

  • Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests The temple's best-known building, built in 1420, 38.2 m high and 24.2 m across.
  • Imperial Vault of Heaven and the Echo Wall The hall and side halls that kept the tablets used at the sacrifices, in a courtyard ringed by a circular wall known as the Echo Wall.
  • Circular Mound Altar The open stone altar where the emperor made the sacrifice to heaven at the winter solstice. Its slabs, balustrades and steps come in nines or multiples of nine, the number of heaven.

Which way round should I walk?

The park suggests a route for each season. In winter: in at the East Gate, along the Long Corridor to the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests, then the Fasting Palace, the Divine Music Administration, the Imperial Vault of Heaven and the Echo Wall, and out at the South Gate. In spring and summer it starts from the West Gate instead. Allow two to three hours.

How do I get there?

  • East Gate Metro Line 5 to Tiantandongmen, Exit A.
  • West Gate Metro Line 8 to Tianqiao, Exit C.
  • By taxi Show the driver 天坛公园 (Tiāntán Gōngyuán) and the gate you want: 东门 (east gate) or 西门 (west gate).

When is the best time to go?

Early. The park opens at 06:00 (06:30 from November to March), two hours before the halls, so you can walk the grounds while they are full of local people exercising, then be at the Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests when it opens at 08:00. Avoid Mondays unless you only want the park. It pairs well with an afternoon at the Summer Palace; see Beijing in 3 days.
