The east side has the flowering courtyards of the eastern palaces and, further east, the two galleries that need their own tickets. Turn right (east) off the central axis after the Palace of Heavenly Purity. Spots 19, 20 and 21 are inside ticketed areas; the rest are covered by your entry ticket.

16. Palace of Celestial Favor

Known for its pear trees. When they flower in spring, the pale blossom stands out against the strong colours of the buildings; the dates depend on the weather.

17. Palace of Accumulated Purity

Magnolias fill the courtyard. They are best in flower, but their branching shape changes the feel of the space at any time of year.

18. Lingzhaoxuan

An unfinished building in the Palace of Prolonging Happiness (Yanxi Palace), often called the Crystal Palace. The nickname is misleading: it was never finished as a glass palace, and what you see is the surviving structure of a project that stopped. Yanxi Palace was closed for a survey in September and October 2026 and reopened on 1 November.

19. Clock and Watch Gallery

Clocks from the imperial collection, shown in the Hall for Ancestral Worship. Separate ticket: ¥10. Last entry is 16:10 from April to October and 15:40 from November to March.

20. Nine-Dragon Screen

A glazed-tile wall 29.4 metres long and 3.5 metres high, made in 1772 when the Qianlong Emperor rebuilt the Palace of Tranquil Longevity. Start with the yellow dragon in the centre, then follow the other eight across the clouds and waves. It is inside the Treasure Gallery area, so it needs the Treasure Gallery ticket.

21. Treasure Gallery

Gold, jade and jewelled objects from the Qing court, shown in the halls of the Palace of Tranquil Longevity. The ticket also covers the opera and stone drum galleries in the same area. The old pines by the gates here have been nicknamed the Eighteen Arhats. Separate ticket: ¥10. Last entry is 16:10 from April to October and 15:40 from November to March.

Tickets and opening

Everyone enters at the Meridian Gate and leaves by the Gate of Divine Might or the East Glorious Gate. The palace is closed on Mondays, except public holidays. Book your entry ticket online with your passport; see how to book.
