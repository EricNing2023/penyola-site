Forbidden City audio guide options, compared

Last checked: 6 November 2026

What are the options for an English audio guide?

There are four: the museum's own rental audio guide, a human guide, a phone app, or a web-link guide bundled with some tickets. Most visitors are well served by either the official device or an offline app; a human guide is worth it if you want to ask questions.

Is the official Forbidden City audio guide any good?

It is the safe default: the museum's own commentary, in several languages, on a handheld device. Its limits are the queue at busy times and that it follows the main halls, so the smaller details in the courtyards get little time.

Should I hire a human guide?

A good guide is the best way to understand the palace if you want stories told around your questions, and the only option that can steer you through crowds and closures on the day. The museum runs its own English-language tours for groups of one to five: ¥600 for an hour on the central axis, ¥1,200 for two hours with the Treasure Gallery, ¥1,500 for two and a half hours, with gallery tickets extra. Agencies and independent guides are the alternative; agree in advance whether tickets are included and how long the tour lasts.

Which apps cover the Forbidden City in English?

Several apps on the App Store cover the Forbidden City in English. They differ mainly in whether the content is stored on your phone and in how it is organised. Check that the app works offline before you rely on it: mobile data from a foreign SIM in China can be slow or unreliable. Read recent reviews too, since some city guides cover the palace in only a few stops.

Penyola, our own app, is one of them. It is built around a hand-drawn map of the palace on which you tap any hall, gate or carved detail to read its story or hear it narrated in English. It suggests walks with times, and keeps the visitor information to hand. Everything is stored on the iPhone, so it works with no signal. The Forbidden City and Summer Palace guides are unlocked together with one purchase of US$6.99; the Jingshan Park guide is free. What it does not do: it does not use your location, so it will not play automatically as you walk; it is in English only; and it is for iPhone only.

Which option should I choose?

  • You want no planning and no phone: the official audio guide.
  • You want someone to ask: a human guide.
  • You want to choose your own pace and keep it after the visit: an offline app.
  • You are short of time: any of them, on the half-day route.

Whichever you choose, bring headphones, a charged phone for your ticket booking and the passport you booked with. If you have not booked yet, see Forbidden City tickets for foreigners.
